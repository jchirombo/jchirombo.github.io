Research · Teaching · Collaboration

James ChiromboStatistics for global health.

Lecturer in Biostatistics · Liverpool School of Tropical Medicine

I apply statistical methods to health research, with a focus on spatial modelling of the health impacts of climate change and human mobility. I am a Lecturer in Biostatistics in the Department of Clinical Sciences at Liverpool School of Tropical Medicine and a Biostatistician at the Malawi Liverpool Wellcome Research Programme in Blantyre, Malawi.

01 / Research

Questions that drive my work.

01

Spatial statistics & climate

Using spatial statistical methods to model the health impacts of climate change, including the climatic and geographic drivers of malaria.

02

Human mobility

Modelling human mobility to support the study of health and infectious disease patterns.

03

Infectious disease epidemiology

Applying statistical methods across malaria, tuberculosis, HIV, and other infectious diseases, in collaboration with clinical and public health researchers.
